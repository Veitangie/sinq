# Community

`sinq` is developed by one person, in the open. Here's where to find the project and how to take part.

## Ask, report, suggest

* **[Discussions](https://github.com/Veitangie/sinq/discussions)**: questions, ideas, sharing how you use `sinq` and what you've built on top of it, including your forks.
* **[Issues](https://github.com/Veitangie/sinq/issues)**: bug reports and feature requests. A minimal scenario that reproduces the problem gets it fixed fastest.
* **Security problems**: please don't open a public issue. Follow the [security policy](https://github.com/Veitangie/sinq/blob/main/SECURITY.md) instead.

## Contributing

* **Documentation**: contributions are welcome. Open a pull request against the `docs/` folder. Documentation is licensed under the GPLv3 like the rest of the repository, and your changes are too.
* **Code**: pull requests aren't accepted at the moment. [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/Veitangie/sinq/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) explains why.

## Forks

`sinq` is GPLv3, so you're free to fork it, change it and share your version, as long as you share its source under the same license. [Discussions](https://github.com/Veitangie/sinq/discussions) is a good place to tell others about your fork.

## Related projects

* **[sinq-helper](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=Veitangie.sinq-helper)**: VS Code extension for `.sinq` and `.scenario` files.
* **[tree-sitter-sinq](https://github.com/Veitangie/tree-sitter-sinq)**: syntax highlighting for Neovim and other tree-sitter editors.
